About the Book

A coming-of-age
in verse.

Pehli Baarish — the first rain — is the moment a young heart learns its own name.

Yatendra Chandra's debut collection moves through the small, unforgiving weather of adolescence: the boy at the window who learned a girl's name by counting her footsteps; the long monsoon afternoons of unsent letters; the slow discovery that the inside of a person can be a country worth writing toward.

54 poems in Hindi, with English titles and translations. Written between 2013 and 2015, in pencil, in the margins of school notebooks.

It is, in the author's words, "a book to be read slowly — preferably during rain, preferably with the lights low."

Read three poems

A small excerpt,
before you decide.

"मैं कविता लिखता हूँ,
तुम कविता जीती हो।
शब्द मेरे हैं, भाव तुम्हारे,
और कविता हमारी।"

"I write the poem.
You live it.
The words are mine, the feeling yours,
and the poem — ours."

Poem 27 · Pehli Baarish

"वो बारिश थी,
जो मेरे भीतर
पहली बार रुकी।"

"It was a rain
that stopped, for the first time,
inside me."

Poem 04 · Pehli Baarish

"उसने नाम पूछा था,
और मेरी ज़बान
बारिश हो गई।"

"She asked my name,
and my tongue
turned into rain."

Poem 19 · Pehli Baarish

"मैंने जो नहीं कहा,
वो भी मेरी कविता है।
शायद उसमें
तुम सबसे ज़्यादा हो।"

"What I did not say
is also my poem.
Perhaps it is the place
where you live the most."

Poem 38 · Pehli Baarish
