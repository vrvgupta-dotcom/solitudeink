Yatendra
The Author Yatendra Chandra

A quiet voice
writing slowly,
listening longer.

Yatendra Chandra is a Hindi poet writing at the soft edges of memory, devotion, and the inner weather of a young country.

He began writing at fifteen, in the margins of school notebooks, between geometry and the rain. The First Movement is a collection of work composed slowly between 2013 and 2017 — three books for three voices that lived in him before he knew their names.

He lives in Bombay, keeps a journal in pencil, and reads aloud only at night.

Writing Philosophy

"A poem is a slow door. You don't open it. You stand near it, until it remembers you."

Years of Work

"Four winters, three monsoons, one quiet hand. The dates on each book are not when they were finished — they are when they let me go."

The Object Craft

A book is a thing
made with the hands.

Every aspect of the collector edition has been considered for permanence — cloth that ages well, paper that does not yellow, ribbons that mark not pages but hours of return.

01 · Cloth

Hand-bound spines

Linen-cotton blend, dyed in indigo, moss, maroon — chosen to age in your hand.

02 · Slipcase

Foil-stamped sleeve

Heavy board, dressed in ivory cloth and pressed by hand with antique gold.

03 · Page

Cream natural paper

Warm to the eye, soft to the thumb. A page made to be returned to.

04 · Marker

Silk ribbon bookmarks

One per book, in a colour chosen for its voice. Sewn into the binding by hand.
