Why this matters

Some books are printed.
This one is pressed — once.

आत्म-मिलन – आत्म-विरह is not a title we will reprint. It is a finite edition, numbered by hand, made in small pressings and held in small numbers. When the last set is claimed, the press closes the book. There is no second run, no anniversary reissue, no paperback to follow. The number on your set is the number it will always be.

We make it this way on purpose. A universe that took five years to write is not meant to be everywhere. It is meant to be somewhere — on one table, in one home, returned to on the quiet evenings.

Pressed once. When the last set is sold, the universe closes.

Ten books · one journey

Ten movements of one
inward weather.

The universe opens with a first rain and closes with a slow melting. Between them, eight more movements — devotion, liberation, the body, separation, the philosophy of love, its many moods, a second language, and the long return of memory. Read in order, they are not ten books. They are one life, learning to feel itself.

The Author Yatendra Chandra

A quiet voice,
writing slowly,
listening longer.

Yatendra Chandra is a Hindi poet writing at the soft edges of memory, devotion, and the inner weather of a young country.

He began at fifteen, in the margins of school notebooks, between geometry and the rain. आत्म-मिलन – आत्म-विरह is the work of four winters and three monsoons, composed between 2013 and 2017 — ten books for the voices that lived in him before he knew their names.

He lives in Bombay, keeps a journal in pencil, and reads aloud only at night.

Writing Philosophy

"A poem is a slow door. You don't open it. You stand near it, until it remembers you."

Five Years of Work

"The dates on each book are not when they were finished — they are when they let me go."
